In 30 seconds

  • Managers need numbers like output, downtime and alarms, not Ladder rungs.

  • A dashboard turns those records into simple charts and key figures.
! Simulated data: no real plant is connected. Read more

This lesson covers: simulated oil-palm plant data, persistent SQLite storage, and management KPIs

Why dashboard matters

Ladder helps engineers debug logic, but management needs production truth: FFB processed, CPO output, OER, downtime, alarm frequency, energy, quality, and repeated bottlenecks. A dashboard turns OT signals into decisions without asking managers to read PLC rungs.

Boundary

This is still simulated. The local historian uses SQLite and seeded oil palm samples. Real Modbus TCP, OPC UA, MQTT, ERP, MES, and pySerial/RS-232 connections come after this local analytics foundation.
