The idea in one sentence

Every lift, traffic light and factory conveyor is run by a small computer called a PLC. This hub teaches how it works, step by step, in plain language.

You do not need a real machine, special software or an engineering degree. Everything is simulated, so mistakes are safe and free.

1. Why this hub exists

the problem it solves

Real equipment is expensive

A training PLC kit with sensors and motors can cost thousands. Most learners never get to touch one.

Mistakes on real machines are risky

A wrong rule can start a motor at the wrong moment. Here you can break things safely and try again.

Most material is full of jargon

This hub starts with machines you already know. Each technical word appears only after the plain idea.

2. The big picture: senses, brain, muscles

every automated machine works this way

On / off or "how much"

A button is simply on or off. A temperature changes smoothly. Engineers call these digital and analog signals.

4. How a PLC works in 3 steps

repeated about ten times every second
  1. Read. Check every button and sensor. Is the door closed? Is the tank full?
  2. Decide. Follow the written rules. "If the door is closed and Start is pressed, run."
  3. Act. Switch motors, lamps and valves to match. Then go back to step 1.

That is the whole secret. The Beginner guide explains each step with a washing machine, a lift and more.
