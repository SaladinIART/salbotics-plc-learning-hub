The big picture

Think of a machine like a body: senses, brain, muscles.

Inputs are its senses. The PLC is its brain. Outputs are its muscles.

On / off or "how much"

A button is simply on or off. A temperature changes smoothly. Engineers call these digital and analog signals.

How it works

Every PLC repeats 3 simple steps.

It does this about ten times every second, for years.

  1. Read

    Check every button and sensor. Is the door closed? Is the tank full?

  2. Decide

    Follow the written rules. "If the door is closed and Start is pressed, run."

  3. Act

    Switch motors, lamps and valves to match. Then go back to step 1.

That is the whole secret. The Beginner guide explains each step with a washing machine, a lift and more.
